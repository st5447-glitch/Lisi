CEFR Level Alignment Matrix

CEFR Communicative Scope Typical Grammar & Output
A1 Familiar everyday expressions, basic personal details, simple interactions. To be, present simple, can/can't, basic adjectives, short sentences and forms.
A2 Routine tasks, past experiences, immediate environment, simple comparisons. Past simple, going to/will, comparatives, basic modals, short narrative paragraphs.
B1 Independent travel situations, justifying opinions, narrating events and plans. Present perfect vs. past, 1st/2nd conditionals, passive voice, connected opinion texts.
B2 Fluent spontaneous interaction, defending viewpoints, abstract and concrete topics. 3rd/mixed conditionals, modals of deduction, relative clauses, structured essays and debates.
C1 Implicit meanings, flexible academic/professional communication, diplomacy. Inversion, cleft sentences, nominalisation, hedging, detailed reports and syntheses.
C2 Effortless mastery, nuanced shades of meaning, rhetorical precision. Stylistic variation, dense academic/literary structures, sophisticated argumentation.
